# Research Claim Audit

This document audits every major research claim in TrustAML-FL against actual experimental evidence. Claims are classified as **SUPPORTED**, **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**, or **NOT SUPPORTED**.

---

## Claim 1: "Federated learning improves AML detection compared with isolated local models."
**Classification: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**

Evidence: Under IID conditions, FedAvg achieves PR-AUC ~0.83 (mean across seeds 42, 123, 456), which is comparable to or slightly above individual local bank models. However, the improvement is not uniformly large across all seeds and conditions. Under severe Non-IID, FL fails completely (PR-AUC = 0.00), demonstrating that the benefit is conditional.

---

## Claim 2: "Increasing institutional non-IID heterogeneity reduces FedAvg performance."
**Classification: SUPPORTED**

Evidence: Multi-seed experiments show IID PR-AUC mean = 0.835, Moderate Non-IID mean = 0.564, Severe Non-IID mean = 0.000. The degradation is monotonic and severe. This is a genuine, reproducible finding.

---

## Claim 3: "FedProx mitigates some degradation caused by client heterogeneity."
**Classification: NOT SUPPORTED (in our experiments)**

Evidence: Under moderate Non-IID, FedProx achieved PR-AUC mean = 0.481 vs FedAvg mean = 0.564. FedProx did not consistently outperform FedAvg and showed higher variance (std = 0.230 vs 0.036). Under severe Non-IID, both methods collapsed to 0.00. This may be due to the small number of clients (3), limited rounds (5), or the specific graph topology.

---

## Claim 4: "Increasing privacy noise produces a measurable privacy–utility tradeoff."
**Classification: SUPPORTED**

Evidence: Weak DP, Medium DP, and Strong DP configurations produce distinguishable PR-AUC values that decrease with increasing noise multiplier, confirming a tradeoff exists.

---

## Claim 5: "Robust aggregation (Median) reduces degradation caused by malicious client updates."
**Classification: SUPPORTED**

Evidence: Under 30% malicious clients with model-replacement attacks, FedAvg PR-AUC degrades severely while Coordinate-wise Median maintains substantially higher detection performance. The delta-based attack correctly targets the gradient update rather than absolute weights.

---

## Claim 6: "Drift-aware adaptation reduces temporal performance degradation."
**Classification: SUPPORTED**

Evidence: Static model PR-AUC ranges from 0.62 to 0.88 across temporal windows. Drift-aware (retrained) model consistently achieves 0.87–0.97, showing clear recovery after temporal shifts.

---

## Claim 7: "The framework uses real financial institution data."
**Classification: NOT CLAIMED — CORRECTLY**

All data is explicitly synthetic, generated by `src/data/generator.py` with controlled AML injection patterns (structuring, circular, fan-out, cross-border).

---

## Claim 8: "The system provides formally guaranteed differential privacy."
**Classification: NOT CLAIMED — CORRECTLY**

Epsilon is reported as `not_computed`. The system implements gradient clipping and Gaussian noise injection, but does not claim a formal (ε, δ)-DP guarantee because a validated RDP accountant for federated heterogeneous graph message passing is not implemented.

---

## Claim 9: "This work is novel for financial fraud detection."
**Classification: NOT CLAIMED — CORRECTLY**

The contribution is described as an *integrated evaluation framework* combining heterogeneous AML graphs, cross-institution FL, non-IID heterogeneity, privacy mechanisms, malicious-client robustness, temporal drift, and graph-oriented explainability — not as a singular novel method.

---

## Claim 10: "The system is production-ready or regulatory-compliant."
**Classification: NOT CLAIMED — CORRECTLY**

The system is explicitly described as a research prototype evaluated on synthetic data.
